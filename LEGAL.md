Not legal advice. If you plan to publish, distribute or flash anything, consult

## What this repository is

Independent interoperability research on a camera the author owns. It documents
file formats, offsets, hashes and observed behaviour. It contains **no Nikon
firmware, no decrypted images, no extracted modules, no XOR tables and no
signing keys**. It contains no flashing instructions and no modified images.

## Copyright

Nikon firmware is copyrighted. This repository does not reproduce it. Offsets,
hashes and short factual constants are recorded for interoperability and
research; they are not a substitute for the firmware.

## Anti-circumvention

Firmware integrity mechanisms can be protected by anti-circumvention law, for
example DMCA section 1201 in the United States and Directive 2009/24/EC in the
EU. The scope and exceptions vary by country. This is why the K8 value and the
XOR tables are **not** published here, even though the method is described.

If you are in a jurisdiction where this research is restricted, do not use it.

## No warranty, no flashing

a warranty and require paid repair. The author provides no recovery method and
